About

I'm a Senior Front-End Engineer with 7+ years of professional experience building enterprise, government and SaaS web platforms with Angular for clients across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt.

I specialize in scalable front-end architecture with Nx monorepos, modern Angular (Signals, Standalone Components, Deferrable Views), RxJS and NgRx, and reusable component libraries.

My background in UI design drives pixel-perfect, responsive and accessible interfaces, delivered in close collaboration with UI/UX and backend teams in Agile environments.
